Who I Am

Hi, I'm Nelvin.

I'm an Operations Manager and Amazon SEO specialist who also builds the automation systems that make operations run themselves. From 2024 to 2026, I managed end-to-end Amazon and e-commerce operations for a catalog of 25,000+ active ASINs at RSH Décor — owning listing optimization and SEO, Seller Central troubleshooting, and day-to-day leadership of an 11-person remote team.

In that time, I raised the account's SEO/listing quality score from 2.8 to 8.7, and built an automation app that turned a full month of manual Flat File work into about a week. In the company's internal performance system, I consistently ranked #2 company-wide — behind only the owner — making me the top-ranked employee on the team.

Building with AI has become a core part of how I work. Using Claude Code and the Claude API, I've shipped a phone-first app that reads receiving paperwork and fills in the details automatically, a suite of 69 live production dashboards, and a full impact portfolio site for a youth program's TAYO Awards entry, handling everything from the database and sign-in to deployment.

I hold a Bachelor's degree in Chemical Engineering (Cum Laude) from Mindanao State University–Iligan Institute of Technology, which shaped how I approach problems: analytically, systematically, and always looking for the system behind the task.

I'm now looking for a remote role where I can bring that same mix of operational discipline, e-commerce expertise, and automation thinking to a growing team.
