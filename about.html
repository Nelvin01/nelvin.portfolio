About Me

A developer who learned the business first: I ran operations and Amazon listings, then started building the software to run them.

Who I Am

Hi, I'm Nelvin.

I'm an App & System Developer. I take slow, manual, error-prone processes and turn them into software people actually use: AI-powered apps, document generators, internal tools, and live dashboards. I build with Claude Code, the Claude and OpenAI APIs, Supabase, Auth0, Next.js, Netlify, and Vercel, and I handle everything from the database and sign-in to testing and deployment.

Some of what I've shipped: a Records and Finance System that writes eleven kinds of official youth council documents as print-ready Word files; a Receiving Digitizer where Claude reads shipment paperwork and fills in the form; an Amazon Flat File app that cut a month of listing work to about a week; and 69 live production dashboards that replaced hard-to-read Airtable reports.

What makes my work different is where I came from. From 2024 to 2026 I managed Amazon and e-commerce operations at RSH Décor: a catalog of 25,000+ active ASINs, Seller Central troubleshooting, and an 11-person remote team. I raised the account's listing quality score from 2.8 to 8.7 and consistently ranked #2 company-wide in the internal performance system, behind only the owner. I also served as Chairperson of the New Pandan youth council, handling its budgets, records, and reports. So when I build a system, I already know the job it has to do.

I hold a Bachelor's degree in Chemical Engineering (Cum Laude) from Mindanao State University–Iligan Institute of Technology, which shaped how I approach problems: analytically, systematically, and always looking for the system behind the task.

I'm looking for a remote role as an App/System Developer or Automation Developer, where I can build tools that save a team real time. I'm also glad to bring my Amazon listing and operations experience to the right team.
