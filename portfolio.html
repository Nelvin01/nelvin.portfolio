Amazon Flat File Automation App

Creating and updating Amazon Flat Files for new listings and product variations was a fully manual, repetitive process — prone to errors and slow to turn around at scale.

What I built: an end-to-end application using Claude Code that automates Flat File creation — generating accurate, ready-to-upload files for new listings and variations without manual rebuilding each time.

Why it mattered: I was the only person on the team who built something that removes future work rather than just completing tasks in front of me — a system that keeps paying off every week instead of a one-time fix.

1 month → 1 week Turnaround time for Flat File-based listing creation (~75% faster)

Amazon Listing SEO Overhaul

Result: raised the account's SEO/listing quality score from 2.8 to 8.7 by the time I left the role — a direct reflection of stronger search visibility and listing quality across the catalog.
